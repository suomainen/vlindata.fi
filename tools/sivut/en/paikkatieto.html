GIS services – from datasets to working information management

I offer municipalities the production, updating and analysis of GIS datasets, and expert help with questions about systems, registers and information management.

The service can be a single dataset update, building a new dataset or a broader development project.

{{ikoni:layers}}

Producing new GIS datasets

Do you need a GIS dataset that does not exist yet?

I design and produce new GIS datasets from, for example, maps, plans, register data, surveys and other source material.

I take the structure, purpose and future maintenance of the dataset into account from the production stage onwards.

{{ikoni:refresh-cw}}

Updating GIS datasets

I update, supplement and correct existing GIS datasets.

The work can include adding new features, completing attribute data, correcting locations and harmonising datasets.

{{ikoni:scan-search}}

Spatial analysis and data processing

I carry out data integration, register comparisons, regional analyses, and digitising and vectorising of datasets.

I also help convert data into different file formats and check data quality.

{{ikoni:database}}

GIS systems and databases

I help with questions about storing and maintaining GIS data, databases, interfaces and the shared use of systems.

The goal is that data is found in the right place and is available wherever it is needed.

Expert help and developing ways of working

Not every GIS problem needs a new system.

I help municipalities assess their current practices and find appropriate solutions for managing datasets and registers.

We can consider, for example, responsibilities for dataset maintenance, database solutions, reducing overlapping work or the flow of information between systems.

Expert help can be a single conversation, a limited study or a larger assignment.
