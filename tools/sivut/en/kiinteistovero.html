Property tax audits and building register quality

Incomplete building data can mean lost property tax revenue for a municipality. VLiNdATA helps identify gaps in building registers and investigate properties whose data needs to be checked.

Request a quote {{ikoni:arrow-right}}

What does the audit include?

A property tax audit combines and compares building register data, property tax data, aerial images and other GIS datasets.

The work can include:

  • Identifying buildings missing from the registers
  • Comparing building locations and attributes
  • Investigating differences between the building register and property tax data
  • Identifying incomplete and conflicting data
  • Classifying the properties to be checked
  • Carrying out enquiries to owners
  • Field checks and reporting of results
  • Annual reporting of property tax revenue

How does the cooperation proceed?

  1. Mapping the starting point

    Together we go through the state of the municipality's building register, the available data and the goals of the work. We agree on the scope, schedule and responsibilities of the audit.

  2. Comparing datasets and investigating

    I identify gaps through register comparisons and spatial analyses. Where needed, the data is supplemented with enquiries to property owners and field checks.

  3. Results and next steps

    The municipality receives a summary of the findings and details of the properties to be checked. The results can be used to update the building register and to plan further investigations.

  4. Clear reporting and support for next steps

    I compile a clear report of the results for officials and elected representatives that makes the benefits of the project visible and supports decision-making. I also help plan and carry out the next steps.
