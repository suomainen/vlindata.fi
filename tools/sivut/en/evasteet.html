How does my site track you?

Cookies are small text files that websites can use to make the user experience more efficient.

Cookies on this site

By law, I may store cookies on your device if they are strictly necessary for the site to work. For all other types of cookies I need your consent.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity cookies are stored only if you accept them. Google Analytics counts visitors. Clarity shows how the site is used: click heatmaps and session recordings in which text typed into forms is masked. If you decline, the page does not load either service at all.

The contact form stores nothing: it opens the message in your own email app.

Privacy: Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity

If you accept cookies, I use Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics to see how the site is used. Clarity records clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, which produce heatmaps and session recordings. Google Analytics counts visitors and shows, for example, which devices they use and where they come from.

I use this data only to improve the site's content and usability, not for advertising or to identify individual visitors. Text typed into forms is masked in recordings and is not stored.

The data is collected with cookies and similar technologies (see the table above). Microsoft and Google process it as service providers, and it may also be processed outside the EU. More information: Microsoft Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab) and Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the Cookie settings button. Tracking then stops and the site's cookies are deleted.
