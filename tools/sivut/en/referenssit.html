Municipality of Aura – investigating building register data

VLiNdATA has worked with the Municipality of Aura to identify and investigate gaps in building register data.

The aim of the work is to improve the quality of building data, promote its correction and explore opportunities to increase property tax revenue.

The audit proceeds in cooperation with the municipality, and the follow-up of its results is developed according to practical needs.
