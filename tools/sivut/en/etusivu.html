Property tax audits

Are all buildings in the registers?

Incomplete building data can mean lost property tax revenue for the municipality.

I help identify buildings missing from the registers, compare building and tax data, and investigate incorrect or incomplete records.

The audit can be carried out in stages to suit the municipality's needs and resources.

More about property tax audits {{ikoni:arrow-right}}

How the municipality benefits

  • An opportunity to increase property tax revenue.
  • More reliable building registers.
  • Less investigative work for the municipality's own staff.

Preparedness

The map works even when the interface does not respond.

When an external interface or network connection goes down, the municipality still needs its map – for example in building control, land use and during disruptions.

A local backup system keeps a local copy of the datasets the municipality has chosen, which can be used in the browser and in QGIS even during an outage.

  • {{ikoni:wrench}}

    Deployment in the municipality's environment.

  • {{ikoni:life-buoy}}

    Maintenance and follow-up of monitoring.

  • {{ikoni:shield-check}}

    Support for continuity planning and preparedness.
