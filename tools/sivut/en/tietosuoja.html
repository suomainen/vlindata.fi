Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data VLiNdATA Oy processes in its own operations, why and for how long, and what your rights are.

Controller

VLiNdATA Oy (Business ID 3410053-6)

This notice covers VLiNdATA's own operations: customers, enquiries, marketing and the website. When I process a municipality's data in an assignment, the municipality is the controller and the data is processed according to the municipality's privacy notice and the data processing agreement. This is described on the Trust and security page.

What data and why

Purposes, data and legal bases

Providing personal data is voluntary, but without contact details I cannot reply to an enquiry or make an agreement. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

Where the data comes from

The data comes from you (email, phone call, contact form, meetings), from your employer in connection with an agreement and from municipalities' public websites. Analytics data is collected on the website if you accept cookies.

Who receives the data

Data is not sold or disclosed for marketing. The following process data on VLiNdATA's behalf:

  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365: email and files, stored in the EU)
  • an accounting firm (invoicing and accounting)
  • Google and Microsoft (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, only with your consent)

Data may also be disclosed to authorities when required by law.

Google Analytics and Clarity data may also be processed outside the EU and EEA, for example in the United States. Such transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Customers and assignments: for the duration of the customer relationship and three years after it ends.
  • Enquiries: three years from the last contact, unless they lead to a customer relationship.
  • Marketing: until the data becomes outdated or you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Accounting: as required by the Finnish Accounting Act (vouchers six years, financial statements and books ten years).
  • Analytics: cookie lifetimes are listed on the Cookies page. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to

  • know what data about you is processed and get a copy of it
  • ask for incorrect data to be corrected
  • ask for your data to be deleted or its processing restricted
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, including marketing
  • transfer data you provided to another system when processing is based on a contract or consent
  • withdraw your consent at any time (analytics: the switch on the Cookies page).

Send requests to virpi@vlindata.fi. I will reply within one month.

If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman: tietosuoja.fi (opens in a new tab).

How data is protected

Data is kept on an encrypted work computer and in Microsoft 365 behind two-step authentication. Only those who need the data for their work have access to it. The practices are described in more detail on the Trust and security page.
