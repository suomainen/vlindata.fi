How can I help?

I am a seasoned GIS professional with over 20 years of experience. I run projects systematically and do hourly work as agreed. Get in touch and tell me what you need – I will work out the best solution. :)

References

  • GIS datasets

    Creating GIS datasets from surveys, source data and analyses makes work easier, and I make sure the data stays easy to use and maintain later on – by yourself, if needed. I am happy to digitise local detailed plans, shore plans and master plans, which makes them easier for the municipality to use. Information that only exists "in employees' heads" should also be made available to everyone and kept up to date.

  • Database analyses

    Databases hold a vast amount of information, and combining it gets more value out of the data collected. Linking the data to location also helps to see the bigger picture.

  • Property tax audits

    Property tax is a significant source of income for municipalities. Buildings that are missing from the municipal and tax authority registers, or whose details are incomplete, can be found and brought within the scope of taxation. The project can be divided into parts: register comparison, spatial analysis and a questionnaire. If needed, I can also help organise the field measurements.

About me

I am a mother of three young people, the spouse of a part-time farmer, a GIS manager in my day job, a part-time entrepreneur and a landlord. Many irons in the fire, in other words.

Running everyday life takes strong organisational skills, and project management is one of my strengths. I make sure schedules are kept and data is well managed, and I support my clients as needed – sometimes holding their hand. :)

I master data collection, editing, conversions and analyses. I always aim to offer my clients the best possible solution for their needs. My goal is to help and make work easier.

The young people in our family do the company's fieldwork, and giving them work experience is important to me. When needed, I draw on my wide network of partners to achieve the best result.
