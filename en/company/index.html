Practical GIS expertise

Hi, I'm Virpi Lindvall!

I'm the founder of VLiNdATA Oy and a GIS specialist with over 20 years of experience in the field.

I have worked widely on the challenges of municipal GIS, registers, building control and land use.

In municipal work I have seen how much data and how many different tasks municipalities manage with limited resources. There is not always enough time to develop and maintain everything.

Through VLiNdATA, I want to help municipalities with these challenges.

I enjoy exploring data, solving problems and turning separate datasets into a working whole.

I work systematically and flexibly. It matters to me that the client gets the help they need and that cooperation runs smoothly.

When needed, I gently nudge officials forward towards the goal we have agreed on together, and I am even ready to stand on one hand so that together we find the best solution.

When needed, I also draw on my partner networks and other experts.

My aim is to make things easier, not more complicated.
