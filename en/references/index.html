Smooth cooperation with municipalities

Municipality of Aura – improving the quality of building register data

Long-term cooperation to bring building registers up to date and improve the information base for property taxation.

VLiNdATA Oy has worked with the Municipality of Aura since 2024. The aim of the work is to identify gaps in the building registers, investigate building data and promote bringing it up to date. Reliable building data supports the municipality's operations and helps ensure that property taxation is based on data that is as complete and correct as possible.

The audit proceeds in stages

  1. 2024

    Register comparison and spatial analysis

    The cooperation began with a comparison of the building register and property tax data and a spatial analysis. Combining the datasets identified buildings and properties whose data was incomplete or conflicting and that required closer investigation.

  2. 2024–2026

    Field checks

    Fieldwork has been used to verify that buildings exist and to check the accuracy of the register data. The field observations complement the results of the register comparison and spatial analysis.

  3. 2025–2026

    Property owner enquiries

    Enquiries sent to property owners have gathered additional information about buildings and their attributes. The enquiries support the investigation of incomplete data and the updating of the building register.

The goal: permanently better building data

The audit has been carried out over several years, taking the municipality's resources into account. Proceeding in stages makes it possible to target the investigations, check the data and update the registers in a controlled way.

The work creates the conditions for growth in property tax revenue, improves the reliability of building data and supports information management in the municipality's building control.
